# Film category preview

Local, publish-ready copy of the Film Horizon + contact sheet + case mockup.
No live push is included. Open index.html from a static server.

This package is the complete current Film set: the 28 films from the earlier preview, plus Weird Kid, plus the eight films that had been held out (Cover With Love · Isabelle, No Place Like Home, Explorers, Flower Crown, Buddy Time: Grandpa’s Story, Mandie, The Smell Needs To Go, and Reel 2026). Gill approved the full set for this preview on 12 November 2026 (America/Vancouver). Titles, credits and posters come from the existing site film pages and filmdata.js. Nothing was invented.

37 posters are included. Clips are not included; every entry is a poster only.

## Gestures
- Tap the standing poster to open it in the case (no pinch required).
- Swipe left or right to move between films.
- Spread your fingers on the Horizon to open the contact sheet.
- Close your fingers (pinch in) on the contact sheet to leave it, back to the Horizon.
- The sheet is not a dead end.

No private photo filenames, no YouTube or Vimeo links, no invented titles or credits.
