# u3 rulebook

A copy of the Sketch Artist guidelines, with Gill’s three corrections.

1. The public bio has no “(he/him)”. Vancouver only. The locked About layers are the words on the calculator.
2. Sound keeps the dotted star-chart waveform. Peaks are labelled with the song, and the current one is lit mint. Duration pills use the length of the audio file.
3. “Nothing orbits” means no two-finger spin. One-finger drift or tilt in Sound stays.

Stars may frame music only. Sound’s dotted waveform and its deepest “among the stars” level stay. Stars do not frame photos, films, or objects.

## How it moves

The work leads. Below the sky, the work gets more of the phone than the stars. One camera. Pinch to go deeper. Pinch out reverses in that same order. Every zoom grows from the spot you touched.

The journey stays: touch the water, the plunge with the near-lens bokeh, then the sky, the category, that category’s board, and only then the piece. A tap on a category opens the category, not a full-bleed still. The board is the clear grid. A pinch grows from the spot you touched. Back goes up exactly one level. The Weird Kid frames bloom into a mosaic, a frame can push until it is full-bleed, the About glitch builds with depth, the moon-grid exit, the minimap, and the glyph shortcuts stay.

Motion is the same with Reduce Motion on. The page does not read `prefers-reduced-motion`, and it does not branch on it. The slow push and the waveform dot pulse run either way.

## The boards keep what worked

From the u3-preview publish on, every fix follows this. The sky, the motion, and the zoom from the touch come into each category: how you move, the layout, and how much sits on the screen. What made the earlier boards work stays with them.

The boards stay clear and readable. The players stay fully usable: the mint audio player, and the film players. Lists stay plain. The work stays dense and fills the phone.

Each category is checked against u2 and site-v3-pro2 at iPhone size. Where this sky is less clear or less dense, the earlier board’s clarity comes back, in this sky’s look.

## What may be on screen

Warm paper is `#e4ddd2`, not white. Low glare. Fonts are Julius Sans One, Poppins, Caveat for notes, Architects Daughter for headings. Those already in the sky stay.

Real media only. No invented copy, no invented credits, no YouTube. Approved family photos only: IMG_5722, IMG_1187, IMG_6881. Files under 25 MB. Media lives in this folder. Pages work with JavaScript off.

## Depth

Film can go deepest. Every other medium has at least four levels and a real bottom.

- Photo: loose stacks, contact sheet, one print, grain crops of that same print.
- Sound: star chart, the player, inside the waveform, then among the stars as the audio moves.
- Objects: the board, the object close-up, process and detail, then a full-bleed joio clip with Spirit Eye beside it.
- About: the calculator, seven layers, then the moon grid.

A full-bleed picture is a middle layer when the chapter still has a level under it. If a piece has no clip, the still pushes slowly. Nothing is invented to fill a gap. An empty level says so, quietly.
