# u4-opus · m4

m4 is a copy of the published m3, with the three fails from the review fixed. m3 started from an exact copy of u3 and follows Gill's RULEBOOK. `CHECKLIST.md` lists every RULEBOOK line as pass or fail, with a screenshot for each.

## What m4 changes from m3
1. **Captions.** The "Just The Beginning" role line reads "Gill James and Ryan". Board captions now cut only between whole words ("Cover With Love · Isabelle…"). A single word that's too long shrinks its type to fit instead of breaking mid-word.
2. **ᑕᐣᓯ.** At every deep level the mark now shows beside ‹, as it does at FAR. One tap from MID, a board, a work or deeper inside a work flies straight to FAR with `history.go(-n)`. Back history stays the same length. Pinch-out, ‹, Esc and Back still go up one level each.
3. **Minimap.** Boards keep a bottom safe area: the board window ends 8 px above the minimap, so no still, print or object tile sits under it.

## What is the same as u3
- The far sky, header, camera, bloom, water, parallax, fly-through and minimap.
- Motion is the same with Reduce Motion on or off.

## What changed
1. **A MID level.** Pinching a chapter first stops on its constellation at u3's fit×1.45. It shows small thumbnails and one-line titles that never collide.
2. **The board** is one more push of the same camera, painted in the sky:
   - Film: 2.39 strips, staggered 2-up.
   - Photo: rotated prints with cream mats and gaps.
   - Objects: the 14 pro2 objects plus Spirit Eye, then Gill's Nov 9 pieces.
   - Sound: one dotted star-chart with every song's peak labelled.
   - About: text rows.
3. **History.** Every step in is one history entry. Pinch-out, ‹, Back and Esc all go up one level.
4. **Fixes:** C1 (hash), C2 (black first frame), C3 (clipped labels), C4 (minimap over type) and C6 (sound labels and covers).
5. **Nov 9 media** (`media/nov9/`) are placed on the Objects, Film and Photo boards. See the end of `CHECKLIST.md`.
