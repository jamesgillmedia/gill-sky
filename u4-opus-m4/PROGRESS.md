- `_spec/DESIGN-SPEC.md` is the source of truth; `RULEBOOK.md` is older and loses where they conflict.

- The first published m4 stays recoverable at main `2d77656`. No frozen copy.
- Never touch the gill-sky root, any other folder, or the gilljames repo (read-only reference).
- Real media and copy only; no YouTube; no `prefers-reduced-motion` branches; no private/held files (spec section 4); never vidA.mp4 after 9 s; the bio is verbatim.

### I. Pinch-out on the deeper bio layers (Gill, 8:59 AM; standalone fix)
- Cause (three together, all only on the scrolling layers 4+ and the moon, where `#laytext` is `overflow:auto` + `pointer-events:auto`):
  1. `#laytext` is a scroll container, so touch-action restarts at `auto` there (the `#stage{touch-action:none}` doesn't reach inside it). A one-finger scroll on the text makes the browser take the gesture and fire `pointercancel`; on iOS Safari a pinch there also becomes a page zoom (user-scalable=no is ignored).
  2. The pinch is driven only by window pointer events. Once the browser owns the touch (after the `pointercancel`), the second finger never gets a `pointerdown`, so the pointer pinch never sees two fingers and a pinch after any scroll does nothing.
