# Objects multi-angle ring package

Source: 162 joio photos from Gill’s November 9 Drive drop (local intake at `/workspace/intake-nov9`), plus two scoop stills from the older photography archive that are not in that drop.

Export: each pick is a 1200px long-edge JPEG (quality 82) and a 480px webp thumbnail, with EXIF and GPS stripped. Nothing was pushed to a repo.

Picks were made by looking at the photographs, not just the catalogue captions. Catalogue descriptions were sometimes wrong (for example the scoop was captioned as a paddle, and a wooden paddle was captioned as a dark cup).

### Wrong object or not useful for a ring

- **Paddles and utensils**, not ceramic objects: 3E21DF64 (catalogued as “hand holding a dark cup,” actually a wooden paddle), IMG_0166, IMG_0167, IMG_2404, IMG_2837, and the rest of the paddle set.

- **1FE894DB**: a charcoal drawing, not a photograph of an object.
- **F3283192**: pale product cup on a white ground, side view. The live objects sketch currently lists this in the dark-glazed ring as “side, lighter.” It is not a dark-glazed cup. It also does not clearly match the unfired set. Left out of all five rings.
- **Brown-glazed small bowl** IMG_5829, IMG_5831, IMG_5832: a different object (cream exterior, glossy brown interior). Not part of the dark-glazed ring.
- **Teal-rim cup** IMG_5393: a different object.
- **Tall dark pot** IMG_2264: a different, taller vessel, not the small unfired cups.
- **Large vessel close-ups** 19FEFB2B and A28A2B8B: a different large pot, not the ashy process bowls.
- **IMG_9150**: a dark-cup frame with an editor bar visible at the edge; IMG_9147 is the clean version.
- Burst near-duplicates were not padded in: e.g. IMG_5843 / 5882 / 5884 / 5893 / 5902, IMG_1323, IMG_1341, IMG_1279 / 1281–1283, IMG_4404’s neighbours IMG_4427, IMG_4444, IMG_4677 / 4679 / 4680.
