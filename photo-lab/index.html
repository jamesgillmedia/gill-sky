Photo lab

Three new rooms for the photographs. Pick one and walk in. Pinch out, or ‹, to come back.

Darkroom

Under the safelight, slide a sheet into the tray and rock it until the print comes up. Hang it on the line. Switch the light on to see it in colour.

Light table

Every frame laid out in strips on a glowing table. Drag the loupe across them, mark your picks in grease pencil, and lift a frame off the table.

Horizon

All the photographs lined up by their own light, darkest to brightest. Scrub the horizon and the sky turns to match each picture.
